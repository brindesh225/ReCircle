ABOUT RECIRCLE

Waste management, made more human.

ReCircle brings households, collectors and community organizations together in one transparent workflow. This local-first platform is designed to support better coordination and accountability.

Frequently asked questions

Is registration free?

Yes. Households can create an account and submit pickup requests in this demo.

Can I track my pickup?

Yes. Your dashboard shows the status timeline and, after collection, the actual quantity and proof photo.

Which materials are supported?

Paper, plastic, glass, metal, e-waste and organic waste.

How do collectors join?

Collector accounts are created and managed by the NGO administrator.
